# BS-ARCH — Map & Operational Layer
## BlueSky PRO — Formalized Architecture

### 1. Purpose
The Map is an operational instrument, not a competing application interface. Its visual language shall be consistent with BlueSky HMI.

### 2. Map capabilities
- Interactive 2D map.
- 3D terrain/map mode.
- Satellite mode.
- Zoom, pan, rotate, tilt.
- Search.
- Flexible scale indicator.
- Actual position display.
- Route editing and altitude editing.
- Point selection and context menu.
- Drawing/editing local restriction zones.
- Display of airspace restrictions, prohibited zones and NOTAM-derived zones.
- Display of weather and correction layers where data are available.

### 3. Provider-independent architecture
```text
BlueSky
   |
Map Service
   |
   +-- 2D Map
   +-- Terrain
   +-- 3D
   |
Provider Adapter
   |
   +-- Provider A
   +-- Provider B
   +-- Local Data
```

The map-data provider shall not be a critical dependency of the core Mission/HMI/Flight/Safety functions.

### 4. Local Data
Map data shall be capable of being loaded into Local Data when entering the interface and retained for later use. Offline/degraded operation shall use locally available data.

### 5. Operational Style
BlueSky shall have its own operational visual layer over the base map:
- route;
- UAV;
- waypoint;
- boundaries;
- zones;
- NOTAM;
- weather;
- corrections.

A JSON-based styling plugin shall be supported, with a configuration panel. A dedicated tablet style shall also be supported.

### 6. Map states
- Planning
- Validating
- Ready
- Flying
- Paused
- Emergency
- Completed

### 7. Route rendering
Routes generated by the optimizer shall be rendered on the map and remain manually editable.
- Move waypoint horizontally and vertically.
- Preserve the new coordinates/altitude after movement.
- Support mouse interaction and tablet interaction.
- When a UAV is selected, show only that UAV route.
- When the mission is selected, show all mission routes simultaneously.
- Show route in 3D space with a projection onto the surface.
- Projection line and projection plane shall be semi-transparent.

### 8. Waypoint interaction
On point selection, display a point window containing:
- point number;
- coordinates;
- altitude;
- manual coordinate entry;
- manual altitude entry;
- displacement controls: left/right/up/down in metres;
- add point;
- delete point.

Right-clicking any waypoint shall open the same operational waypoint menu.

A seven-point offline test stub shall be available:
`DEPARTURE -> WP1 -> WP2 -> WP3 -> WP4 -> WP5 -> WP6 -> WP7 -> DESTINATION`.

### 9. Collision and terrain hazards
- Collision points shall be shown in red.
- Terrain obstacles and dangerous terrain exceedances greater than 15 m shall be displayed with obstacle/elevation information.

### 10. NOTAM and restrictions
Display:
- restriction/prohibited zone boundaries;
- zone number;
- altitude restrictions;
- coordinate-defined boundaries;
- vertical limits.

Selecting a zone shall open a NOTAM window containing:
- NOTAM number;
- source content used to construct the zone;
- close control.

### 11. Route and UAV presentation
The UAV shall be represented by a moving volumetric arrow.
Selecting the UAV icon shall open settings for:
- shape;
- color;
- transparency;
- custom aircraft image.

The completed path and future path shall have separate display settings:
- color;
- width;
- transparency.

Direction indicators shall be placed along the route between waypoints. Their settings shall include:
- color;
- transparency;
- line width;
- arrow/indicator length.

### 12. Waypoint common style
Global waypoint display settings:
- radius;
- color;
- line width;
- fill;
- transparency.

### 13. Map typography
Font sizing shall scale proportionally with map scale and use the project's knowledge-base rules.

### 14. Configuration persistence
Display/configuration changes shall:
- save automatically;
- persist between sessions;
- support rollback to the last successfully saved configuration.

### 15. Operator-created local restrictions
The operator shall be able to create a local restriction zone for optimizer use:
- circular by radius; or
- polygonal from selected map points;
- defined by coordinates and altitude;
- saved after creation;
- intersections between restriction zones shall not be allowed.

Default visibility is local to the operator who created the restriction. A separate control shall allow the restriction to become visible to all operators/users.
