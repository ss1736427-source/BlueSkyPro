# CAP-014 — 3D Reconstruction

## Purpose

Provide three-dimensional reconstruction of mission-relevant terrain, objects or environments.

## Description

3D Reconstruction shall transform approved spatial observations into a traceable three-dimensional representation.

## Inputs

- imagery;
- depth data where available;
- navigation state;
- sensor pose;
- map/terrain data;
- mission context.

## Outputs

- 3D representation;
- point/feature data;
- reconstructed geometry;
- spatial quality information;
- provenance.

## Preconditions

- required observations available;
- coordinate reference established;
- applicable sensor calibration available;
- configuration validated.

## Constraints

- sensor geometry;
- observation coverage;
- spatial resolution;
- processing capacity;
- environmental conditions.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Perception;
- Mapping;
- sensor/payload;
- spatial data.

## Algorithms

Specific photogrammetry, depth, reconstruction or SLAM algorithms remain detailed-design decisions.

## Safety Constraints

Safety-relevant spatial products shall preserve source, version, coordinate and quality context.

Invalid reconstruction shall not silently become authoritative spatial truth.

## Interfaces

- sensor interface;
- navigation interface;
- perception interface;
- mapping interface;
- spatial data interface.

## Performance

Spatial accuracy and reconstruction latency shall be defined by the applicable mission profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- valid reconstruction;
- incomplete coverage;
- sensor degradation;
- coordinate mismatch;
- moving objects;
- noisy observations;
- corrupted data;
- reconstruction failure.

## Rationale

3D Reconstruction extends Mapping with volumetric/spatial reconstruction while retaining separation between source observations and derived spatial products.
