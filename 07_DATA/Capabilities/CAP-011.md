# CAP-011 — BVLOS

## Purpose

Provide controlled mission operation beyond direct visual line of sight where the applicable operational, regulatory, communication and safety conditions are satisfied.

## Description

BVLOS shall coordinate mission execution requirements associated with remote operation, communication continuity, navigation integrity, situational awareness and applicable operational constraints.

## Inputs

- mission;
- mission revision;
- route;
- UAV state;
- navigation state;
- communication state;
- C2 state;
- telemetry;
- airspace data;
- geofence;
- environmental data;
- operational profile.

## Outputs

- BVLOS readiness;
- communication validity;
- operational state;
- constraint status;
- degraded state;
- evidence.

## Preconditions

- approved mission;
- validated configuration;
- applicable BVLOS capability available;
- required C2/communication path available;
- navigation valid;
- applicable airspace and operational constraints satisfied.

## Constraints

- communication coverage;
- latency;
- navigation integrity;
- airspace;
- geofence;
- weather;
- energy;
- platform limitations;
- applicable operational requirements.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Communication;
- C2;
- Telemetry;
- Safety;
- Readiness;
- Scheduling;
- UAV capability.

## Algorithms

Specific navigation, link-management and contingency algorithms remain detailed-design decisions.

## Safety Constraints

Loss or degradation of required communication, navigation or situational-awareness conditions shall produce explicit degraded or blocked state.

BVLOS shall not bypass Safety Engine or Execution Authority.

## Interfaces

- C2 interface;
- communication interface;
- navigation interface;
- telemetry interface;
- airspace interface;
- safety interface;
- readiness interface.

## Performance

Communication freshness, latency and navigation continuity targets shall be defined by the applicable operational profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- normal BVLOS operation;
- communication degradation;
- communication loss;
- navigation degradation;
- stale telemetry;
- airspace constraint change;
- weather degradation;
- contingency;
- recovery;
- unsafe resume prevention.

## Rationale

BVLOS is modeled as an operational capability assembled from existing communication, C2, navigation, safety and readiness boundaries rather than as an independent execution authority.
