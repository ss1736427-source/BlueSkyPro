# CAP-006 — Inspection

## Purpose

Provide systematic inspection of mission-relevant assets, areas or conditions.

## Description

Inspection shall execute an approved inspection pattern and collect observations required to evaluate the target according to mission-defined criteria.

## Inputs

- mission inspection definition;
- target/area definition;
- navigation state;
- perception data;
- sensor/payload data;
- spatial constraints;
- environmental data.

## Outputs

- inspection observations;
- inspection coverage;
- inspection findings;
- inspection status;
- evidence references.

## Preconditions

- mission revision identified;
- inspection configuration validated;
- required sensors/payload available;
- navigation capability ready;
- applicable safety constraints satisfied.

## Constraints

- inspection area;
- altitude;
- route;
- sensor characteristics;
- environmental conditions;
- UAV capability;
- communication constraints.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Perception;
- Mapping;
- applicable Detection/Tracking;
- telemetry;
- mission execution.

## Algorithms

Inspection planning and analysis algorithms are not fixed at architecture level.

## Safety Constraints

Inspection shall respect mission, geofence, airspace, altitude and platform safety constraints.

Incomplete or invalid inspection evidence shall not be represented as complete inspection.

## Interfaces

- mission interface;
- navigation interface;
- perception interface;
- mapping interface;
- sensor/payload interface;
- execution interface;
- evidence interface.

## Performance

Coverage, latency and inspection quality targets shall be defined by the applicable mission profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- complete inspection;
- partial inspection;
- route deviation;
- missing observations;
- stale observations;
- sensor failure;
- communication degradation;
- recovery.

## Rationale

Inspection is a mission-level capability composed from navigation, sensing, perception and evidence generation without binding the architecture to a particular inspection algorithm.
