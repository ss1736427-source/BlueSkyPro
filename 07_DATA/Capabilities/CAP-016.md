# CAP-016 — Environmental Monitoring

## Purpose

Provide environmental observations required for mission planning, execution, monitoring and safety decisions.

## Description

Environmental Monitoring shall collect, normalize, validate and expose relevant environmental conditions while preserving source, timestamp and freshness.

## Inputs

- weather observations;
- weather forecasts;
- temperature;
- wind;
- visibility;
- precipitation;
- atmospheric conditions;
- applicable external environmental data;
- onboard observations where available.

## Outputs

- environmental state;
- observations;
- forecast data;
- freshness state;
- validity;
- warnings;
- historical environmental record.

## Preconditions

- applicable data sources configured;
- source identity available;
- time context available;
- applicable data validation available.

## Constraints

- source coverage;
- update frequency;
- forecast uncertainty;
- communication;
- sensor availability.

## Dependencies

- External Data;
- Communication;
- Telemetry;
- Data Architecture;
- Mission Planning;
- Safety.

## Algorithms

Environmental aggregation, interpolation and forecasting algorithms are not fixed at architecture level.

## Safety Constraints

Stale, unavailable or invalid environmental information shall remain explicitly identifiable.

Environmental Monitoring shall not independently authorize unsafe operation.

## Interfaces

- external data interface;
- sensor interface;
- telemetry interface;
- mission interface;
- safety interface;
- monitoring interface.

## Performance

Freshness, latency and accuracy targets shall be defined by the applicable mission profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- valid observations;
- stale observations;
- missing source;
- conflicting sources;
- forecast change;
- sensor failure;
- external service failure;
- recovery.

## Rationale

Environmental Monitoring isolates external and sensor-derived environmental information from mission authority while making freshness and uncertainty explicit.
