# AI Concept

The AI function is designed as an internal system capability rather than a chat interface attached to the product.

## Internal learning layer

The system can compare planned and observed operational data:

forecast wind ↔ actual wind
planned energy ↔ actual energy
planned route ↔ operator correction
expected aircraft behaviour ↔ observed behaviour

The resulting corrections become structured operational knowledge for future missions.

## External analysis layer

A separate analytical layer can compare the product's technology and operating environment with external technologies, regulatory developments and competitive capabilities.

## AI operating principle

AI proposes, explains and learns from corrections. Operational authority remains with the defined human/operator control process.

The review package deliberately omits model architecture, training data structures, proprietary scoring, coefficients and implementation code.
