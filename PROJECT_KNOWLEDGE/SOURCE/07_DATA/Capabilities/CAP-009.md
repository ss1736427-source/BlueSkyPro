# CAP-009 — Delivery

## Purpose

Provide controlled transport and delivery of an approved payload to an authorized destination.

## Description

Delivery shall bind payload, destination, route, timing and delivery conditions to the approved mission revision.

## Inputs

- delivery mission;
- payload definition;
- destination;
- navigation state;
- schedule;
- UAV capability;
- environmental data.

## Outputs

- delivery status;
- payload state;
- delivery confirmation;
- location/time evidence;
- deviation state.

## Preconditions

- payload identified;
- destination validated;
- mission approved;
- route validated;
- UAV capability sufficient;
- required safety conditions satisfied.

## Constraints

- payload capacity;
- payload handling;
- destination constraints;
- route;
- geofence;
- airspace;
- weather;
- energy;
- communication.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Scheduling;
- Resource Manager;
- UAV capability;
- execution;
- telemetry.

## Algorithms

Route and delivery optimization algorithms are not fixed at architecture level.

## Safety Constraints

Delivery shall not bypass safety, approval or execution gates.

An unconfirmed delivery shall not be reported as successful.

## Interfaces

- mission interface;
- payload interface;
- navigation interface;
- scheduling interface;
- execution interface;
- telemetry interface;
- evidence interface.

## Performance

Delivery accuracy and timing requirements shall be defined per mission profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- valid delivery;
- destination deviation;
- payload unavailable;
- route deviation;
- communication loss;
- aborted delivery;
- partial completion;
- recovery.

## Rationale

Delivery is modeled as a capability rather than a vehicle-specific function so different platforms and payload mechanisms can satisfy the same mission intent.
