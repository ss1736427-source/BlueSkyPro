# CAP-010 — Drone-in-a-Box

## Purpose

Provide integrated station-based deployment, mission execution support and recovery for compatible UAV operations.

## Description

Drone-in-a-Box shall coordinate station, UAV, mission and operational state while preserving Core authority and safety boundaries.

## Inputs

- mission;
- mission revision;
- UAV state;
- station state;
- configuration;
- weather/environment;
- communication state;
- schedule.

## Outputs

- station readiness;
- UAV readiness;
- launch status;
- recovery status;
- charging/service state;
- operational evidence.

## Preconditions

- station available;
- compatible UAV available;
- mission approved;
- configuration validated;
- communication available where required;
- safety conditions satisfied.

## Constraints

- station capability;
- UAV compatibility;
- weather;
- energy;
- communication;
- airspace;
- geofence;
- maintenance state.

## Dependencies

- Mission;
- Scheduling;
- UAV;
- Fleet;
- Communication;
- Safety;
- Readiness;
- Deployment.

## Algorithms

Station scheduling and automation algorithms are not fixed at architecture level.

## Safety Constraints

Drone-in-a-Box shall not independently bypass mission approval, Safety Engine or Execution Authority.

Automatic launch/recovery shall remain subject to defined safety and readiness gates.

## Interfaces

- station interface;
- UAV interface;
- mission interface;
- scheduling interface;
- readiness interface;
- safety interface;
- telemetry interface;
- execution interface.

## Performance

Availability, launch/recovery timing and service requirements shall be defined per operational profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- station startup;
- UAV insertion/removal;
- launch;
- recovery;
- charging;
- communication loss;
- station failure;
- UAV failure;
- weather degradation;
- power loss;
- recovery.

## Rationale

Drone-in-a-Box is a capability boundary integrating station infrastructure with the existing mission, safety, readiness and execution architecture without creating an independent operational authority.
