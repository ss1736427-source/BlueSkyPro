# CAP-008 — Fire Monitoring

## Purpose

Provide monitoring of fire-related conditions, areas and changes relevant to a mission.

## Description

Fire Monitoring shall collect and interpret applicable observations to identify fire-related conditions, track changes and provide evidence for operator and mission decisions.

## Inputs

- sensor observations;
- thermal or other applicable payload data;
- navigation state;
- map/terrain;
- environmental data;
- mission area.

## Outputs

- fire observations;
- detected fire-related conditions;
- monitored area;
- changes over time;
- evidence references.

## Preconditions

- mission area defined;
- required sensing available;
- applicable environmental and safety constraints validated.

## Constraints

- temperature/environmental conditions;
- sensor range;
- altitude;
- airspace;
- smoke/visibility;
- energy;
- communication.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Perception;
- Detection;
- Mapping;
- Environmental Monitoring where applicable.

## Algorithms

Specific thermal analysis, classification and fire-model algorithms are not fixed at architecture level.

## Safety Constraints

Fire monitoring shall not authorize unsafe flight or override Safety Engine decisions.

## Interfaces

- sensor/payload interface;
- navigation interface;
- perception interface;
- detection interface;
- mapping interface;
- mission evidence interface.

## Performance

Monitoring latency, coverage and detection quality shall be mission-profile dependent.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- known fire condition;
- no-fire condition;
- degraded visibility;
- thermal sensor degradation;
- false detection;
- missed detection;
- changing conditions;
- communication failure.
