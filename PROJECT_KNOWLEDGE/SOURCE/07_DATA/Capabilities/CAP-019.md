# CAP-019 — Obstacle Avoidance

## Purpose

Provide obstacle detection and avoidance support during mission execution.

## Description

Obstacle Avoidance shall use applicable spatial and sensor information to identify collision hazards and provide validated avoidance responses or constraints.

## Inputs

- sensor observations;
- perception;
- obstacle detections;
- navigation state;
- mission route;
- map/terrain;
- UAV state;
- environmental data.

## Outputs

- obstacle state;
- collision-risk state;
- avoidance proposal;
- avoidance status;
- safety condition.

## Preconditions

- applicable sensors available;
- navigation valid;
- obstacle processing available;
- safety configuration validated.

## Constraints

- vehicle dynamics;
- sensor range;
- latency;
- terrain;
- geofence;
- airspace;
- mission constraints.

## Dependencies

- Navigation;
- Perception;
- Detection;
- Mapping;
- Safety Engine;
- Execution.

## Algorithms

Specific avoidance, collision prediction and trajectory algorithms remain detailed-design decisions.

## Safety Constraints

Obstacle Avoidance shall have authority sufficient to protect the defined safety boundary but shall not silently alter mission intent beyond authorized safety behavior.

Unsafe or invalid avoidance results shall not be reported as successful.

## Interfaces

- sensor interface;
- perception interface;
- navigation interface;
- mission interface;
- safety interface;
- execution interface.

## Performance

Reaction latency and avoidance quality shall be defined by platform and operational profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- static obstacle;
- moving obstacle;
- late detection;
- sensor degradation;
- false obstacle;
- obstacle disappearance;
- route conflict;
- constrained environment;
- emergency response;
- recovery.

## Rationale

Obstacle Avoidance provides a reusable safety-critical capability while preserving explicit boundaries between perception, safety decision-making and mission execution.
