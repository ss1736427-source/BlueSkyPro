# CAP-017 — C-UAS

## Purpose

Provide controlled detection, tracking and situational awareness for counter-UAS mission scenarios.

## Description

C-UAS shall integrate applicable sensing, detection, tracking, classification and mission-context information for authorized operational decisions.

This capability does not itself define or authorize a physical countermeasure.

## Inputs

- sensor observations;
- Perception;
- Detection;
- Object Detection;
- Tracking;
- Navigation;
- spatial data;
- mission context;
- environmental data.

## Outputs

- detected object;
- track;
- classification;
- spatial state;
- confidence/validity;
- situational-awareness information.

## Preconditions

- authorized C-UAS mission;
- applicable sensors available;
- configuration validated;
- safety constraints satisfied.

## Constraints

- sensor limitations;
- airspace;
- geofence;
- environmental conditions;
- identification uncertainty;
- mission authorization.

## Dependencies

- Perception;
- Detection;
- Object Detection;
- Tracking;
- Mapping;
- Navigation;
- Environmental Monitoring;
- Safety.

## Algorithms

Specific detection, classification and tracking algorithms remain detailed-design decisions.

## Safety Constraints

C-UAS information shall not automatically authorize engagement or other hazardous action.

Positive identification, uncertainty and confidence shall remain explicit.

## Interfaces

- sensor interface;
- perception interface;
- detection interface;
- tracking interface;
- spatial interface;
- mission interface;
- safety interface.

## Performance

Latency and detection/tracking quality targets shall be defined by the applicable mission profile.

## Verification

Verification shall cover:

- valid detection;
- false detection;
- missed detection;
- multiple objects;
- ambiguous classification;
- track loss;
- sensor degradation;
- environmental degradation;
- communication failure.

## Rationale

C-UAS is modeled as a mission capability focused on authorized sensing and situational awareness, while safety and execution authority remain outside the capability.
