## Review Rules

1. CANDIDATE does not mean accepted.
2. UNMAPPED requirements require architectural review.
3. One SYS-REQ may map to multiple capabilities.
4. One capability may satisfy multiple SYS-REQ.
5. Mapping shall be based on requirement meaning, not identifier proximity.
6. No requirement shall be silently deleted or renumbered.
