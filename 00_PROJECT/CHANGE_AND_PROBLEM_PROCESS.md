# Change and Problem Process

## Status

This document defines the controlled process for recording, evaluating, approving, implementing, verifying, and closing changes and problems within the BlueSky PRO project.

## 1. Purpose

Changes and problems shall be recorded in a controlled manner so that project status, impact, disposition, implementation, and verification remain traceable to the applicable controlled baseline.

## 2. Applicability

This process applies to project-controlled requirements, design and planning records, verification records, configuration-controlled artifacts, and identified problems or nonconformities affecting those records.

## 3. Change control

Each proposed change shall have a unique record and shall identify:

- the affected controlled artifact;
- the reason for the change;
- the source or trigger for the change;
- the impact requiring consideration;
- the disposition and approval status;
- the implementation record; and
- the required verification or re-verification.

A change shall not be treated as complete until the affected controlled records have been updated and the required verification has been identified or completed in accordance with the applicable verification record.

## 4. Problem control

Each identified problem shall be recorded with:

- a unique identifier;
- a clear description of the observed condition;
- the affected artifact or process;
- its current status;
- the disposition or corrective action;
- verification of the disposition where required; and
- closure status.

## 5. Traceability

Change and problem records shall retain linkage to the authoritative master register and the applicable approved baseline. The exact requirement linkage remains subordinate to the authoritative master register and the applicable approved baseline.

## 6. Records

Controlled records generated by this process shall be maintained in the project repository under the applicable controlled-record structure. Repository history provides the implementation record for committed changes.

## 7. Closure

A change or problem may be closed only when its required disposition has been implemented, the affected controlled records are consistent, and required verification evidence or verification status has been recorded.
