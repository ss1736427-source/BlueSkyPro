# ARCH-DEC-010 — Mission Intelligence and Decision Authority

## Decision

BlueSky shall separate mission intelligence, optimization, validation, safety decisions and operator authority.

Mission AI shall assist in transforming user intent into mission plans and decision options.

Mission AI shall not independently authorize real mission execution.

## Mission Intelligence Flow

The primary decision flow shall be:

USER INTENT
→ TASK
→ MISSION CONFIGURATION
→ CAPABILITIES
→ MISSION GRAPH
→ MISSION PLAN
→ VALIDATION
→ OPTIMIZATION
→ SIMULATION
→ READINESS
→ SAFETY GATE
→ OPERATOR APPROVAL
→ EXECUTION

Mission AI may participate in applicable planning, optimization, simulation and adaptation stages.

## Mission AI Responsibilities

Mission AI shall support applicable functions including:

- interpretation of structured user tasks;
- mission configuration;
- template adaptation;
- capability selection;
- mission planning;
- route planning;
- sector planning;
- payload selection;
- resource allocation;
- relay planning;
- mission variant generation;
- optimization;
- explanation of planning decisions;
- adaptation to changed mission conditions.

## Decision Explanation

Mission AI shall provide an explanation for material planning decisions.

The explanation should identify applicable factors such as:

- user requirements;
- mission objectives;
- required capabilities;
- constraints;
- optimization profile;
- available resources;
- validation results;
- simulation results;
- environmental conditions.

## Separation from Safety Engine

Mission AI shall not replace or override the Safety Engine.

The Safety Engine shall remain authoritative for safety-critical decisions.

The following relationship shall apply:

MISSION AI
→ PROPOSE / PLAN / OPTIMIZE

SAFETY ENGINE
→ VALIDATE SAFETY / BLOCK / ALLOW SAFETY-WISE

## Separation from Mission Validation

Mission AI may generate or modify mission candidates.

Mission Validation shall independently evaluate the resulting mission against applicable mission requirements and constraints.

AI-generated output shall not be treated as valid merely because it was generated by Mission AI.

## Separation from Mission Readiness

Mission Readiness shall provide the consolidated readiness state of the mission.

Mission AI may use readiness information as an input.

Mission AI shall not independently declare a mission ready for execution.

## Operator Authority

The operator shall retain authority over decisions requiring operator approval.

The system shall provide the operator with sufficient information to review:

- mission objective;
- selected mission variant;
- optimization profile;
- capabilities;
- validation results;
- readiness;
- safety conditions;
- conflicts;
- simulation results;
- relevant AI explanations.

## Automatic Decisions

The system may perform automatic decisions where the applicable architecture and safety rules permit them.

Examples may include:

- selection of a suitable optimization profile;
- generation of mission variants;
- conflict resolution proposals;
- route alternatives;
- resource allocation proposals;
- adaptation proposals.

Automatic decisions shall remain subject to validation and safety constraints.

## Execution Authority

Mission AI shall not provide a direct execution path to UAV control.

The execution authority chain shall remain:

QML
→ C++ CORE
→ VALIDATION / SAFETY
→ ADAPTER
→ EXTERNAL SYSTEM

The UI and Mission AI shall not bypass the Core, Safety Engine or required validation stages.

## Human Approval Boundary

When explicit approval is required, the mission shall transition through:

READY
→ APPROVAL_REQUIRED
→ APPROVED
→ EXECUTING

Mission AI shall not automatically convert APPROVAL_REQUIRED into APPROVED.

## Replanning and Adaptation

Mission AI may propose adaptation when:

- mission conditions change;
- conflicts are detected;
- validation results change;
- communication conditions change;
- energy state changes;
- weather changes;
- fleet state changes.

A proposed adaptation shall be evaluated by applicable validation and safety mechanisms before becoming an operational mission change.

## Runtime Adaptation

During execution, Mission AI may provide recommendations or candidate adaptations.

The final action shall remain subject to:

- Safety Engine;
- Mission Validation;
- applicable mission rules;
- operator authority when required.

## Traceability

Material AI decisions shall remain traceable to:

- input task;
- mission revision;
- capabilities;
- optimization profile;
- generated alternatives;
- selected alternative;
- validation results;
- simulation results;
- readiness state;
- safety decision;
- operator decision when applicable.

## Authority Hierarchy

The decision authority shall follow:

SAFETY CONSTRAINTS
>
MANDATORY SYSTEM CONSTRAINTS
>
MISSION REQUIREMENTS
>
OPERATOR APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
>
USER PREFERENCES
>
MISSION AI RECOMMENDATIONS
>
OPTIMIZATION PREFERENCES

The hierarchy shall prevent an AI recommendation or optimization objective from overriding mandatory safety or system constraints.

## Rationale

Separating intelligence from authority allows Mission AI to provide advanced planning and adaptation while preserving deterministic validation, safety control, traceability and human approval for decisions that require it.
