# Controlled Disclosure Boundary

This branch is a presentation and technical-review surface.

Included:
- product concept;
- system-level architecture;
- module relationships;
- operational workflows;
- AI principles;
- development direction;
- traceability philosophy.

Excluded:
- source code;
- proprietary algorithms;
- exact optimization formulas;
- coefficients and calibration parameters;
- model weights and training datasets;
- internal schemas required for reproduction;
- credentials, keys and production configuration;
- unreleased implementation details.

This boundary is deliberate: the project can be technically understood and evaluated without receiving the material needed to reproduce its implementation.
