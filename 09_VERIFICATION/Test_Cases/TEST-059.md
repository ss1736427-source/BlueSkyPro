# TEST-059 — AI Solution Justification Inspection

## Objective

Verify [[SYS-REQ-097]] by inspection of the AI solution-generation and decision records for persistent solution justification.

## Preconditions

- AI solution-generation mechanism or decision records are available.

## Inspection Criteria

For each proposed AI solution, verify that a persistent justification contains:

- detected problem or improvement opportunity
- source data
- factors used
- expected improvement mechanism
- expected result
- anticipated risks
- limitations
- alternative solutions
- reason for selecting the recommended solution

## Expected Result

Each proposed AI solution has a persistently stored justification containing all required elements.

## Result

Not Run

## Evidence Required

- AI solution records or decision records.
- Stored justification associated with each proposed solution.
- References to source data and alternatives.
